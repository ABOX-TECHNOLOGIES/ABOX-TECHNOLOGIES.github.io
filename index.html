Before you start

Good questions.
Clear answers.

We’re new to AI. Where do we begin?

Start with one process that takes time or makes information hard to find. We can help assess its fit for AI and define a manageable first project.

Can we keep using our existing tools?

We start by looking at the tools you already use. Where access and integrations allow, we connect AI to your existing systems and agree any changes with you.

How do you approach sensitive business data?

Data access, hosting, model choice, and human oversight are part of the initial assessment. We agree the requirements with you before implementation.
